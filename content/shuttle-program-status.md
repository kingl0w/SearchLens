The status of the space shuttle phase of the space transportation system is reviewed by the use of a series of graphs and slides. Briefly described are: space shuttle capabilities and configurations; launch vehicles; mission profiles; recovery concepts; cost comparisons; program schedules; and benefits of reusable space transportation system.
