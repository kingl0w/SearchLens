The Margin Unit of Jezero crater, Mars, was identified from orbit as one of the most carbonate-rich regions of the planet [e.g., 1,2]. Its presence, along with the adjacent fluvial delta [e.g., 3] made Jezero crater the most compelling landing site for the Mars 2020 mission. Investigation of Jezero’s Margin-Unit carbonates provides a unique opportunity to address the formation of carbonates in sedimentary deposits, possibly under a CO<sub>2</sub>-rich martian atmosphere. Here we report on chemistry and mineralogy of 55 targets observed by the multi-technique SuperCam instrument during Perseverance’s crater-margin campaign.
