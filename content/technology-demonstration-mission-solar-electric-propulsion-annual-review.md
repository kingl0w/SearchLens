The Solar Electric Propulsion (SEP) project is developing and
qualifying an advanced 12 kW Electric Propulsion (EP) thruster to
the Power and Propulsion Element (PPE) requirements, which are
applicable to human/robotic exploration and commercial spaceflight
missions.
