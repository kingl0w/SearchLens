The Phase II CARR (Customer Acceptance Readiness Review)  Project Apollo CSM 012 Phase II Report consists of: Systems Assessment Minutes, Systems Assessment Responses, CARR Minutes, CARR Responses.  These four sections are prepared and transmitted progressively as soon as available. The sections, when bound together by the recipient, constitute the total Phase II Report, and when combined with the Phase I report become the updated CSM 012 CARR report required by contract NAS9-150, Exhibit R.
