This document is the accumulation of reports and assessment letters prepared by the Stafford Task Force/Anfimov Advisory Expert Council Joint Commission during Phase 2 of the International Space Station. The Joint Commission performed critical reviews of the activities leading up to, and during the initial assembly of the ISS Building on the experience of the Joint Commission during Phase 1, the Joint Commission is pleased to be able to present the achievements accomplished during Phase 2 We will continue to monitor and assess the ISS' operational readiness to ensure safe and reliable operation of the space station in the future.
