NASA’s Space Launch System (SLS) vehicle was  successfully  launched on  November  16,  2022, initiating the Artemis I mission to send the uncrewed Orion spacecraft to the Moon and further into space  than  any  human-rated  spacecraft  had previously  flown. Flight  certification  of  the  launch vehicle  was  part  of  the  systems  engineering  and  integration  (SE&I)  effort  conducted  by  NASA  to successfully certify the mission and  begin  a  new  era  of  human  space  exploration  of  the  Moon  and eventually Mars. This paper describes the SLS Program flight certification approach including the organization,  processes, and  reporting. This  approach  used  traditional NASA  SE&I  milestone reviews and lessons learned from the Space Shuttle Program, as well as unique methods to certify the complex system, procedures, and personnel readiness. The approach utilized by the SLS Program to conduct flight certification for the Artemis I mission can be extended to address flight certification for other complex programs.
